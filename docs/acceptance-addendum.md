# Accepted scope and design deviations

Integration update, 2026-10-23. The [independent AI-assisted review](independent-employer-review.md) accepts Actuator Lab as a bounded synthetic, one-axis numerical/control artifact. The original private workspace retains its accepted bytes. This public package makes documented setup/text transformations; [publication provenance](publication-provenance.json) distinguishes those changes from the unchanged numerical source and historical outcomes.

The independent reviewer reran 27 C++ tests, 32 Python tests and core/simulation ASan+UBSan checks. The owner experiment record contains 121 gates. A fresh binary exactly reproduced 213 recorded trajectories/summaries (21 named and 192 Monte Carlo runs). A separately authored absolute-coordinate oracle checked 32 new constant-forcing cases at two resolutions and 48 intervals around scheduled discontinuities. All 192 Monte Carlo success/trip/abort/excursion flags were unchanged at four-times-finer integration. See [independent results](../evidence/independent-review-results.json), [test log](../evidence/independent-review-tests.txt) and [original manifest](../evidence/manifest.json).

On the same frozen 64 synthetic inputs, direct voltage control succeeds in 0/64, feedforward in 10/64 and cascaded current feedback in 64/64. The feedforward ablation was added after observing the direct/cascade results, with no resampling or retuning of those inputs. It is a labeled follow-up, not preregistration or proof of an optimal controller. Failures under sustained torque, voltage loss, stuck drive and increased drag remain in the record. Neither these counts nor the soft speed gate establishes population reliability, a continuous-time safety guarantee or physical hardware performance.

**Design deviation:** proposed settling-time, gate-occupancy and separate gross supplied/regenerated energy summaries were not delivered. Current structured outputs instead provide terminal-window maxima, limit/abort outcomes and balance defects; traces provide gate flags and signed cumulative electrical work. Net electrical work is not separate supplied and regenerated energy. The implemented acceptance gates do not depend on those extra metrics, but the complete original metric plan must not be described as fulfilled.

This accepted scope excludes reinforcement learning, orbital mechanics, general electromagnetics, three-dimensional spacecraft dynamics, calibrated physical parameters, hardware drivers, physical actuator tests and flight qualification. AI-assisted artifacts do not establish personal mastery or employer endorsement.
